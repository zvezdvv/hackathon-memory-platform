# Guide: capture a process

Complements the interview basics. Goal: a repeatable workflow with trigger, steps,
people involved, deadlines and pitfalls.

## Questions in this order

1. **Trigger:** What starts the process? (event, date, report, weather situation …)
2. **Goal:** How do you know it was completed successfully?
3. **Steps:** "What do you do first? And then?" – go through to the end.
   For every step, ask:
   - Who does it? Who has to be informed or approve?
   - Which documents, forms or systems are needed?
   - How long does it take, which deadlines apply?
4. **Branches:** "What if …?" – typical deviations and escalations.
5. **Pitfalls:** Where do mistakes happen? What is often forgotten?
6. **Legal basis:** Which regulation is the process based on? (cross-check with
   `search_knowledge` and link it)

## Additional content for the protocol

```markdown
## Workflow
| # | Step | Who | Deadline / duration | Tools |

## If-then rules
```
