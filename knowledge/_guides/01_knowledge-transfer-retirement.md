# Guide: knowledge transfer before retirement

Complements the interview basics. Goal: make everything that only exists "in the head" of
the departing person usable for their successor.

## Topic blocks (spread over several sessions)

1. **Priorities & routines**
   - What always takes priority in your area – and why?
   - Which tasks come up at the same time every year / season?
2. **Places & special cases**
   - Which places, objects or cases need special treatment?
   - Where has something gone wrong before? What did you learn from it?
3. **People & network**
   - Who do you call when …? (name, role, best way to reach them)
   - Who decides what, including informally?
4. **Decisions under uncertainty**
   - How do you decide whether to act or to wait?
   - Which rules of thumb do you use that are not written down anywhere?
5. **Legal & liability**
   - In which situations does it get legally tricky?
   - What must be documented immediately?
6. **Outdated knowledge**
   - What in the official documents is no longer true?
   - What has changed in recent years?

## Good closing question for every session

> "If your successor were on their own tomorrow – what is the one mistake they should
> avoid at all costs?"

## Notes

- For places, always ask for the exact designation (street, section, house number).
- Do not store private phone numbers for contacts – only role and official channel.
- Keep each session to 20–30 minutes at most; several short sessions are better.
